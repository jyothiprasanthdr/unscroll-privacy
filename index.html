This Privacy Policy explains how Unscroll ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information. Unscroll is an iPhone app that locks the apps you choose when you reach a daily limit, and lets you open them for a short time after you finish a small mission.

The App is developed and operated by Nandha Kumar Ilangovan ("the Developer"), who is responsible for your information under this Policy.

By installing or using Unscroll you agree to this Privacy Policy and to the Terms of Use. If you do not agree, please do not install or use the App.

1. The short version

  • No ads, no selling, no tracking: We show no ads, never sell your data, and do not track you across other apps or websites. We never collect Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • Your apps stay private: Apple's Screen Time does the locking. Apple does not tell us the names of the apps you pick, and we never see them. We store only a sealed Apple token for each one.
  • Camera and motion are live only: Rep-counting missions read the camera, and the Steps mission reads motion data, on your device in real time. Nothing is recorded, saved, or sent.
  • Your words stay on your phone: What you write in a reflection or an if-then plan is stored on your device only and is never uploaded.
  • Sign-in is optional: You can use Unscroll as a guest. Signing in with Apple or Google only backs up your guards and history so they follow you to a new phone.
  • Full account deletion: You can permanently delete your account and cloud records any time in the App under Settings > Account > Delete account.

2. Information we collect and process

We collect only what is needed to lock your apps, unlock them fairly, and keep your history.

2.1 Information stored on your device

  • Guards: The limit you set for each guarded app, weekend limit, quiet hours, whether a guard is frozen, and the sealed Screen Time token that identifies the app to iOS.
  • Unlock records: Which mission you completed, when, for how long the app opened, and the effort level for that day. Unlocking works offline from this record.
  • Reflections: The feeling you named, the sentences you write, and your if-then plan. Device only.
  • Preferences: Mission difficulty and targets, unlock length, notification and haptics choices, and onboarding answers.

2.2 Account information (only if you sign in)

As a guest, Google Firebase Authentication gives your install a random anonymous identifier. If you choose Sign in with Apple or Google, it also processes:

  • A unique pseudonymous user identifier (Firebase UID).
  • Your name, if provided by your sign-in method.
  • Your email address. With Sign in with Apple you may choose Apple's Private Relay to keep your real email hidden.
  • Identity provider metadata (Apple or Google).

2.3 Cloud backup

To restore your guards on a new phone and keep your history, the App stores a copy of your guards, daily usage minutes per guarded app, unlock records, and in-app notices in your private record in Google Cloud Firestore. Database rules allow only you to read or write your own records. Reflections are not part of this backup.

2.4 Screen Time (Apple Family Controls)

Unscroll uses Apple's Family Controls, Device Activity, and Managed Settings frameworks. You choose the apps in Apple's own picker. Apple tells us only that a daily threshold was reached; it does not give us the app's name, your browsing, or what you do inside an app. This data is used on your device to lock and relock apps.

2.5 Camera (processed live, never stored or transmitted)

The Squats, Push-ups, Jumping jacks, and Look up missions use the camera to detect body or head position and count repetitions. Analysis happens on your device in real time. No photo or video is recorded, saved, or uploaded.

2.6 Motion (processed live, never stored or transmitted)

The Steps mission reads your step count from the phone's motion data to verify the mission. It stays on your device.

2.7 Notifications and Live Activities

Alerts, the unlock prompt on a locked app, the optional morning note, and the Live Activity that shows time left in an open window are all scheduled and delivered by your phone. We do not run a push-notification server for them. Alerts count apps; they cannot name them.

2.8 Crash reports

We use Google Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports, which include the crash trace, device model, and iOS version. They are used only to fix problems.

2.9 What we NEVER collect

  • No precise or coarse location.
  • No contacts, photo library, calendar, or microphone.
  • No health records or biometrics.
  • No advertising identifiers (IDFA) or behavioral tracking profiles.
  • No sale or rental of personal information, ever.

3. How we use your information

  • Lock your guarded apps at their limit and open them for the length you chose after a mission.
  • Make each same-day unlock ask a little more than the last.
  • Show your Progress, streak, and history.
  • Back up and restore your guards when you sign in on a new phone.
  • Find and fix crashes.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests.

5. Data storage, security, and retention

Storage: Guards, unlock records, reflections, and preferences live in your iPhone's app sandbox. Backed-up data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore, encrypted in transit and at rest.

Retention: Local data remains until you delete the App. Cloud data is kept while your account is active. When you delete your account, your guards, usage, unlock records, and notices are removed from Firestore and your sign-in is removed from Firebase Authentication. Crash reports are kept by Google for a limited period.

6. Your rights and data controls

  • Use without an account: You never have to sign in to use the App.
  • Delete your account: In the App, go to Settings > Account > Delete account.
  • Revoke permissions: Manage Screen Time, Notifications, Camera, and Motion any time in iOS Settings, or from Settings > Permissions in the App. The related missions or locks stop working; the rest continues.
  • Manage subscriptions: If you subscribe, cancel or change it in Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions.
